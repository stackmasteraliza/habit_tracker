Privacy Policy

Privacy First

Habit Tracker is built with privacy at its core. Your habits, streaks, and personal goals are stored only on your device. We believe your journey to self-improvement should remain private.

Information We Collect

We collect absolutely no personal information.

Habit Tracker stores all data locally on your device:

  • Habit names and descriptions
  • Completion history and dates
  • Streak counts
  • Icon and color preferences

Data Storage

Your habit data is stored securely on your device's local storage:

  • Data never leaves your device
  • No internet connection required
  • No account creation needed
  • No synchronization with external services

Third-Party Services

Habit Tracker operates independently without any third-party integrations:

  • No Analytics: We don't track how you use the app
  • No Advertising: No ads, no ad networks
  • No Social Features: No sharing or social media integration
  • No External APIs: Everything works offline

Permissions

Habit Tracker requests only essential permissions:

  • Storage: To save your habits and progress locally

We respect your privacy and never request unnecessary permissions like camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Your Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Delete habits: Swipe to remove any habit instantly
  • Reset progress: Clear completion history anytime
  • Uninstall: Removing the app deletes all data permanently

We do not retain any backup of your data once deleted.

Children's Privacy

Habit Tracker is safe for users of all ages. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app can help young users build positive habits in a completely private environment.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
